NFL Kickoff is around the corner and we have some great matchups to get us underway!

He Needs the Axe will be looking to start their title defense with a win against the hottest regular-season team last year, The Kushblazers. The Kushblazers, on the other hand, will be out for revenge after suffering a semi-final loss to The Axe in the playoffs last year.

The Slammers host the Hot Night Slugz. HC Brabazon will be hoping to reach the playoffs for the 5th time, whereas HC Dolan will be hoping his Slugz can reach a playoff berth for the first time in their history.

Pogo's Lads narrowly missed out on the playoffs for the first time last year, and now HC Pogie faces a tough matchup against a coach considered around the league to have built one of the best squads this year in Fitzmagic. Is this the start of something special for HC Fitzpatrick in his 3rd year?

HC Ghose vs. HC Kenny — one of the oldest fans of the game against the commissioner of the league. Coach Ghose previously accused the commissioner of corruption, so this should be a fiery matchup.
